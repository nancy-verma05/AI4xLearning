Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), and Deep Learning (DL) are closely related technologies, but they are not the same. 
    AI is the broad concept of making machines intelligent.
    ML is a subset of AI that enables systems to learn from data.
    DL is a subset of ML that uses deep neural networks to solve complex problems. 
    
AI → ML → DL

What is Artificial Intelligence (AI)?

AI is the science of making machines perform tasks that normally require human intelligence. Such as - Reasoning, Decision making, Language understanding, problem solving
Examples:
    ChatGPT
    Alexa
    Siri

What is Machine Learning (ML)?

Machine Learning is a technique that allows computers to learn from data instead of relying only on manually programmed rules.
Instead of telling the computer every rule, we provide examples, and it learns from them.

Example: 
    Email Spam detection
    Fraud Detection
    Product recommendation based on customer preference

What is Deep Learning (DL)?

Deep Learning is a specialized branch of Machine Learning that uses deep neural networks with multiple layers to automatically learn complex patterns from large amounts of data.

It performs exceptionally well for image recognition, speech recognition, and natural language processing.

Example: 
    Face unlock
    Voice assistants


Explanation in Layman terms:

AI: You give the rules.
Example: 
    Dogs have four legs.
    Dogs bark.
    Dogs have a tail.
AI follows those rules.

ML: Instead of rules, you show thousands of pictures of dogs and non-dogs.
The system learns from the pattern and recognize who is dog and who is not.

DL: You show millions of pictures. It automatically learns:
    ears    
    eyes
    nose
    fur
    body shape
without anyone explaining what to look for.
